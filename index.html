About Me

I am an accounting student at the University of Lethbridge with a strong interest in financial reporting, problem solving, and helping businesses make informed decisions. Throughout my studies, I have developed skills in accounting, Excel, and business communication while also gaining experience with technologies like Python, HTML, CSS, and GitHub through course work.

Outside the classroom, I enjoy challenging myself through strongman training and competitions, which has taught me a lot about discipline, perseverance, and setting long-term goals. I am also a husband and father, and my familiy is my greatest motivation to continue learning and growing in my career.

While I have experience working with data analysis tools and technology, my primary goal is to build a career in accounting where I can combine technical skills with financial expertise to help businesses succeed. I am committed to continuous learning and growth, with plans to further my career with CPA and CFP designations. I look forward to contributing to my community through my strogn work ethic and providing valuable business advice and services.
